Delete your Reborn Rising account and data

In the app (fastest):

  1. Open Reborn Rising and go to Me.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password or your Google or Apple sign-in.

Your account and every backup we hold are deleted straight away.

Without the app: Email info@rebornrising.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete your account and all backed-up data within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted: your login, and your backed-up food logs, workouts, routines, body measurements, Stack entries and settings.

What may be kept: crash reports and feedback messages, for up to 12 months, only to fix problems. They're then deleted.

Data saved only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app. Watch readings from Health Connect are never stored by us; you can remove the app's Health Connect permission in your phone settings.
