# Launch stories: how to post

Post them in this order on launch day. They're all 1080 x 1920, with text kept clear of the top and bottom bars.

1. `story-01-its-here.png`: the app is here.
2. `story-02-coach.png`: pick your coach.
3. `story-03-protein.png`: hit your protein.
4. `story-04-muscles.png`: see every muscle.
5. `story-05-ranks.png`: earn your rank.
6. `story-06-code-drop.png`: the code REBORNRISING for 24 hours. Add Instagram's link sticker in the empty space at the bottom, pointing to rebornrising.com/app.

Story 6 is your "code in the story" option. Post it once the APP comments pile up, so everyone who commented gets the code at once. Leave the code out of posts and reels, so it stays a reward for people who watch your stories.

You can add music from Instagram's library. Don't add a poll or question sticker on frame 6; the link sticker is the one action.
