Privacy Policy: Reborn Rising app

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how Reborn Rising ("we", "us") handles information in the Reborn Rising app on Android, iPhone and the web.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we receive your email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we also receive the name on that account. An identifier is assigned to your account.

Information you enter. This includes your food and nutrition logs, workouts and routines, body weight and measurements, goals, and the supplements, medicines and other items you choose to record in Stack. All of it is optional and entered by you.

Crash reports. If the app stops working, it sends a technical report to help us fix the problem. The report contains the error details, the app version, the device and browser type, and your account identifier.

Feedback. If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, any contact detail you choose to add, and technical details about the app version and device.

Apple Health and Health Connect (only if you turn them on). If you connect your watch, the app reads your steps, calories burned, heart rate and weight from Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android, with your permission, and shows them in the app. These readings stay on your phone and are not sent to us, except a weight you choose to add to your log, which is then part of your backup. The app never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect. You can remove this permission at any time in your phone's Health settings or Health Connect settings. We never sell this data, use it for advertising, store it in iCloud, or share it with anyone, and our use of Health Connect data follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to provide the app's features, back up and restore your data across your devices, fix problems, and reply to feedback. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. Where information is stored

Your entries are stored on your device. When you are signed in, an encrypted backup is kept on Google Firebase (Google LLC), which acts as our service provider. Information is transferred over encrypted connections and stored encrypted. Our database rules stop any other account from reading your backup. We access stored data only when that's needed to maintain the service or to respond to your request.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Health information

The app is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it does not recommend doses. Information in the app is educational. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your backup for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all backed-up data at any time in the app under Me > Account > Delete account, or by following the steps at https://www.rebornrising.com/delete-account. Deletion is permanent and takes effect immediately. Crash reports and feedback are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.

7. Age

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

10. Contact
